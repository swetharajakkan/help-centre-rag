Account security

A customer who has lost access to their two-factor authentication device can reset it by using one of the ten backup codes that were shown when two-factor authentication was first enabled. If the customer has no backup codes left, support must verify their identity with a video call and a government ID before the two-factor setting can be removed from the account.

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and are checked against a list of known breached passwords when they are set. After 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row, the account is locked for 15 minutes, and the account owner receives an email listing the location of each failed attempt.

Only the account owner can change the email address on an account, and the change is confirmed by a link sent to both the old and the new address. The link expires after 24 hours, after which the request has to be started again from the security settings page.
