# Refund Policy

Customers who cancel an annual plan within 14 days of purchase receive a full refund, and the money is returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. After the first 14 days, annual plans are not refunded, but the subscription stays active until the end of the paid term so the customer keeps access to everything they paid for.

Monthly plans are never refunded for a partial month, because the customer can cancel at any time and will simply not be charged for the next month. If a monthly customer was charged after cancelling, support refunds that charge in full once the cancellation date is confirmed in the billing history.

Duplicate charges caused by a payment retry are refunded automatically within 3 business days, and the customer receives an email when the refund has been issued. Support agents should not issue a manual refund for a duplicate charge until those 3 business days have passed, otherwise the customer is refunded twice.
