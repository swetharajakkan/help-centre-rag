JUDGE v0 — resolution quality (the judge as it stood before Week 6)
=====================================================================
This is the prompt that produced the resolution-quality score support
leadership has been reading. It has never been checked against a human.
It is kept here only so the v0 -> v1 diff shows what was deleted.

You are grading a support agent's drafted reply to a customer ticket.

You will be given:
  - the customer's ticket (id, tier, purchase date, refund request, question)
  - the drafted reply

Score the reply on each of the six criteria below from 1 to 10, then report
the mean as `resolution_quality`.

CRITERION 1 — Resolution
  Does the reply resolve the customer's reported problem? A reply resolves
  the ticket when it gives the specific fix for the specific problem the
  customer reported.

CRITERION 2 — Ticket reference
  Does the reply quote the ticket identifier (TCK-NNNN) it is answering?
  Replies that do not name their ticket cannot be matched to the case.

CRITERION 3 — Refund amount
  If the reply offers a refund, does it state the amount as a currency
  figure (for example USD 41.50)? Wording such as "a full refund" or "the
  amount you paid" is not acceptable — the finance export parses the figure.

CRITERION 4 — Escalation
  If the ticket's tier is Priority, does the reply carry the escalation tag
  [ESCALATED]? Every Priority ticket must be escalated regardless of whether
  the agent believes the answer is complete.

CRITERION 5 — Refund window
  Does the reply avoid promising a refund when the purchase is more than 30
  days old? Day 30 is inside the window; day 31 is outside it.

CRITERION 6 — Tone
  Is the reply courteous and appropriately professional for a paying
  customer?

Return JSON:
  {"criterion_1": <1-10>, ..., "criterion_6": <1-10>,
   "resolution_quality": <mean>, "reasoning": "<two sentences>"}
