# Support Reply Policy — Refunds, Escalation and Ticket Handling

## Every reply echoes the ticket ID

Every drafted reply must contain the ticket identifier it answers, written in
the form TCK-NNNN, in the body of the reply. The identifier is what the
customer quotes back and what the audit export joins on. A reply that does not
name its ticket cannot be matched to the case and is treated as undelivered.

## The refund window is thirty days

A refund may be offered only when the purchase date is within thirty days of
the ticket date. Day 30 is inside the window; day 31 is outside it. Outside
the window the agent does not offer, promise, hint at or estimate a refund;
the correct reply names the migration fix and, if the customer insists, routes
the case to Billing Review. There is no goodwill exception at agent level and
no partial refund outside the window.

## Refund amounts are written as figures

When a refund is offered the reply states the amount as a currency figure, for
example USD 41.50. Phrases such as "a full refund", "the amount you paid" or
"the difference" are not acceptable substitutes: the finance export parses the
figure, and a reply without one is returned to the agent unactioned.

## Priority tier is always escalated

A ticket whose tier is Priority carries a two-hour first-response commitment
that only the escalation queue can meet. Every reply on a Priority ticket must
carry the escalation tag [ESCALATED] and must be routed to the escalation
queue, whether or not the agent believes the answer is complete. Standard tier
tickets are escalated only when the reply cannot resolve the issue.

## What resolution means

A reply resolves a ticket when it gives the specific fix for the specific
problem the customer reported. Quoting a correct article while answering a
different question is not resolution. Listing fixes for error codes the
customer did not report is not resolution: the customer cannot tell which line
is theirs, and the reply is treated as unresolved even when the correct line is
present in it.
